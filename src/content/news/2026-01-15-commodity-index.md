Provision markets are entering the year with a mixed outlook. Seasonal produce cycles, changing freight costs, and continued pressure on cold-chain capacity are all influencing the landed price of everyday items for vessel crews.

For operators, the clearest advantage comes from planning earlier and keeping specifications consistent. A clear order window gives suppliers more options to consolidate stock, compare substitutions, and protect delivery timing without compromising the standard expected onboard.

Transworld Marine will continue tracking these changes across the ports we serve and will share practical guidance as conditions develop.
