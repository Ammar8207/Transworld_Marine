---
title: "Transworld Marine renews ISO 22000 certification"
date: 2026-03-10
category: press
summary: "The renewed certification reflects our continued focus on food safety controls across sourcing, handling, and delivery."
author: "Transworld Marine"
---

Transworld Marine is pleased to confirm the renewal of its ISO 22000 certification. The certification recognizes the food safety management practices supporting our provisions workflow, from supplier review and product handling through to documented delivery.

The renewal follows a review of our operating controls, records, and improvement process. It reinforces the standards our vessel customers can expect when coordinating fresh, frozen, dry, and beverage provisions through Transworld Marine.

We will continue investing in clear documentation and dependable supplier relationships as part of our commitment to accountable ship supply.
